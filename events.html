Inktober

Annual art event hosted by creatives () where for the month of October, a one-word prompt is released everyday. Artists around the world (including you!) can partake in this event by creating a creative piece such as an ink sketch, digital illustration or even a doodle inspired off these prompts. Every year, thousands of artists share their creative ideas and interpretations tagging the official Inktober account below!

Artfight

Art Fight is a global art event hosted every (the month) where a growing 850,000 artists come together to play a massive fannart gifting game! It typically features the creation of unique team names and themes for artists to join, and artists can participate by finding artists with original characters of their liking, drawing, modelling or animating a piece of fannart and sending an 'attack'. Creating attacks, defenses, revenges and friendly fire all add up to your personal score that contributes to your team score, and by the end of the month the winning team is announced. You can join too by creating an account on their official website in the link below, and adding your very own original character spreadsheets and descriptions.

Artfight Discord Minigames

Since the rapid growth of Artfight and its community, the Artfight admins host art minigames in their official Discord server when Artfight is off season! This includes small and fun games such as 'Draw the Person Above You' or telephone art, which involves an initial artwork being redrawn multiple times by everyone and seeing all the details change with each piece!

DTIYS

DTIYS stands for 'Draw it in your style' and is a common art challenge hosted by a plethora of big AND small artists online. These challenges come with a character, prompt or idea for you to redesign or create in your own artstyle, and it often comes with some prizes depending on the artist, like a some prize money, a mystery gift from their artshop, or a free commission! You can find many amazing DTIYS events by searching up the #DTIYS on social media platforms like Instagram, X, Tiktok and more.

100 Day Project

Although the '100 Day Project' is not an actual event or a community, it is a prevalent personal art challenge that many artists take themselves up to. It involves creating a single goal or subject/art form that you want to improve at and sticking to it for 100 days! Many artists on social media use it in a way that they post one piece of art every day, working on things like perspective, rendering, anatomy, animals and architecture. Watch yourself get better step by step, and see the rewarding transformation in your art!

Secret Santa

Secret Santa is another art gifting event hosting by hundreds of art Discord servers in the festive season of Christmas (although some art communities do it all year round!). It takes the exciting anonymity of traditional Secret Santa and replaces boxes of presents with gorgeous art based of your wishlist from a fellow community member! You can find discord art servers easily by searching in the communities section for 'art servers'; your favourite youtubers and artists might own a server too!

Art Trades/Collabs

Art trading and art collaborations are both fun and often casual activities you can do with anyone: your school friends, your art mutuals, or even unacquainted artists from a Discord server! Some ideas that are done regularly include sending physical parcels of letters/drawings/trinkets, creating a piece of illustration of eachother's original characters or using a template or art trend where each artist can draw a specific pose or section of the final artwork!

Character Design Challenge

The team at Character Design References runs a popular art design challenge every month that thousands of international artists partake in. It invites artists to submit a single illustration of a character interpreting the monthly theme, where stand-out entries often win prizes such as art books, graphic novels, tutorials, digital brushes and a chance to feature on their homepage! This challenge promotes creative design and allows artists a chance to build their art portfolio alongside a supportive community of artists. You can participate by joining their Facebook Group, tagging them on Instagram, or by submitting on their exclusive Patreon. More information can be found below on their official site.
