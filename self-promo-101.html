Promote Your Art Online:
The 101 Guide

Posting Schedule

Although quantity and consistent posting is important, often people find that posting 2+ times a week can be exhausting, leading to constant burnout and artblock. When you begin posting on social media, you should consider a posting schedule that is most sustainable for YOU.

Social Media Platforms

Posting across multiple platforms, including both social media and art posting platforms, can be beneficial for growing your art presence online much quicker. Many artists find it helps them reach more people and curate a more loyal and diverse audience base!

Consistency

Quality is also important especially for making a consistent and curated art profile that is appealing to users! This doesn't specifically refer to how 'GOOD' your art is: it's about presenting honing into your specific strengths, artstyles or fandoms /communities you want to appeal to

Personality

Engaging with your audience and expressing yourself and your personality is not a waste of time! It was actually found by the IBN that people are 7x more likely to interact with a personality-driven account over a generic page. This is why it's important to reply to comments and make sure your posts have your personal voice!

Content

Modern social media is now increasingly pushing the consumption of reels over posts, which has caused a significant shift in art promotion online. Many artists often find it discouraging to have to make these reels, however you can create some very easily through posting speed paints with trending audios. For those trying to avoid generic formats and hooks, try joining in on some art trends, audios and DTIYS to encourage the platform to push your art to peoples' feed.

Algorithm

Multiple successful artists on social media have stated the importance of paying attention to the algorithm. Pay attention to what works: see what types of videos and posts the algorithm pushes and try to produce similar content. For example, your account could focus on posting fannart from specific fandoms and shows, which helps the algorithm find people who are interested in your content.

Mutuals

Art moots! They are what make posting art on social media fun and exciting: supporting eachother's creations in the comments, collaborating with one another, and talking about your interests with one another. It is much more valuable to have genuine interactions and share love and support than to gain any number of views or likes.

Most importantly,

Don't be discouraged or afraid to experiment! After all, your following does not matter, it is more about connecting with people and artists and sharing your creativity! And at the end of the day, you don't need to strictly follow any of these tips, they are JUST some tips to help support your art account growth journey.
